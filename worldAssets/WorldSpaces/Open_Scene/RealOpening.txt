The date was June 11th 1975.
You were the captain of the DWPV Atlantic.  
It was a little past 10:45 PM
When you looked ahead and saw
An unending wall of rain.
The waves broke high on the ships bow.
You tried to sail through the storm
And saw a lighthouse shining through the dark.
You sailed toward the glow of salvation.
While your radio crackled with the faint sound of music.
Before a wave hit the ship, and everything went quiet.
