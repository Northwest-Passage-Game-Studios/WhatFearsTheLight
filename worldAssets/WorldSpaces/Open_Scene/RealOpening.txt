The Date is June 11, 1975.
You were the chief mate of the DWPV Atlantic.  
It was a wet and cold morning.
But that is normal thing out in the deep.
It was little past 8:45 AM.
When your lookout cried.
Squall Dead ahead.
The waves broke high on the ships bow.
As you try navigating through the storm
You see a lighthouse standing firm the in the gale.
As you stay at the light house.
The radio crackles with faint sound of music.
And a massive wave stricks the ship abeam causing it to brake.
